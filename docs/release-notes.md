ElectroPOS 1.0.0

- First release of the new ElectroPOS, with a web app: sell, buy, take returns and keep products, customers, suppliers, expenses and reports from the phone or from a browser (https://inventory-management-pos.web.app). Both see the same shop.
- Put your shop online from the phone (Cloud screen), then add staff by their Google email as admin, manager or salesperson. Salespeople see only the selling side.
- The phone keeps working without internet. Sales made offline are sent when it comes back, and other devices see them within seconds.
- Coming from an earlier ElectroPOS build that has Backup & Restore: make a full backup there, install this one, restore the backup (Settings → Backup & Restore), then put the shop online. The two apps can sit side by side until you're sure.
- The app updates itself from this repository's releases.

Install the APK below on Android 7.0 or newer.
